01 / The problem

Acquisition keeps getting more expensive. The list you already paid for is underperforming.

You pay every day to bring in new customers. The cost of acquiring a customer rises, CPMs rise, and margins shrink.

You already have a list and customers you paid to acquire. They're generating less revenue than they could. Every month this continues, you leave money on the table.

  • Incomplete, misconfigured or poorly optimized automated flows.
  • Campaigns sent without a clear plan or precise segmentation.
  • A list whose health declines over time, with emails landing in spam more often without anyone noticing.

What I check in your Klaviyo account

01

Account and data

Account settings and the quality of data coming from your store.

02

Automated flows

Welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase: when they trigger, who they go to, what they say, and where they overlap.

03

Campaigns

Who you send them to, how often, what they say, and what they generate.

04

Segmentation

How you group your contacts and whether you communicate differently with frequent buyers, one-time buyers, and people who have never bought.

05

List health and deliverability

Whether emails actually reach your customers' inboxes.

06

Gaps and conflicts

What's missing and where flows and campaigns overlap.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Klaviyo audit cost?

My Klaviyo audit costs €750 as a one-time payment, no VAT on top. I review your entire account to tell you what's not working, why it's costing you sales, and how to get it generating revenue again, with clear priorities. You get a written report and the call recording. If you continue with ongoing management, I deduct the €750 from your first month's fee.

I already want ongoing management - do I still need the audit?

Yes. Ongoing management always starts with the audit. Before I change flows, campaigns or segments, I need to see where the account is underperforming and what to improve right away. That way, we start with the changes that matter most. If we continue together, I deduct the €750 audit fee from your first month's fee.

I don't use Klaviyo yet - does the audit make sense?

Yes, it does. I analyze your current email platform: flows, campaigns, segmentation and list health. I tell you what's costing you sales today. You also get a migration plan to Klaviyo. The price is still €750, which I deduct from your first month's fee if I then manage the account.

I have an in-house team - can you train them?

Yes, on request - we can discuss it on the qualification call. The audit is still the first step, because it gives your team clear priorities to focus on and a report to use as a reference during training.

What do you need to run the audit?

I need access to your Klaviyo account (or your current email platform) and, if available, the connected ecommerce data. Then I review your account and data, automated flows, campaigns, segmentation, list health and deliverability, gaps and conflicts.

What if my account is already in good shape?

If your Klaviyo account is already in good shape, I'll tell you directly on the call. That also saves you unnecessary work. The report covers what I checked and any changes I recommend. You also get the call recording to keep or share with your team.

How much of my time does it take?

Very little. You just need to give me access to Klaviyo and join two calls: the qualification call and the walkthrough call. I deliver the audit within 7 business days of receiving access. You don't need to prepare anything special beyond the requested access.
