4. OpenAI Ads measurement

The OpenAI Ads Measurement Pixel is disabled by default and is activated only after choosing “Accept”. When active, it measures page views and successful lead submissions and may use first-party measurement identifiers, including the advertising click reference. The legal basis is consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

You may withdraw or change your choice at any time through “Cookie settings”. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it. If you reject measurement, the contact form remains fully usable.

5. Local storage and cookies

The site stores oaiq-consent in the browser to remember whether measurement was accepted or rejected. If measurement is accepted, the OpenAI Pixel may set first-party identifiers such as __oppref and __obref for attribution and event matching. These are not activated before consent.
