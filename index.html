03 / Method

My philosophy

The ecosystem approach

A well-built retention program isn't just about emails - it's a strategic blueprint that connects acquisition, product intelligence, omnichannel relationships, and post-purchase stability into a single, high-performing system.

01

Paid media alignment

Synchronizing Klaviyo segments with Meta and Google ads to lower CAC and suppress existing customers from top-of-funnel spend.

02

Product intelligence

Leveraging zero and first-party data to predict next-purchase timing and specific product recommendations based on behavior.

03

UGC and social proof

Automating the collection and deployment of customer sentiment at the exact psychological moment of peak satisfaction.

04

Multi-channel orchestration

Seamlessly blending email, SMS, WhatsApp and push notifications to meet customers where they actually engage, without over-saturation.

05

Post-purchase experience

The "second sale" happens in the delivery phase. We optimize every touchpoint from tracking to unboxing to re-order.

06

Lifetime value as the north star

The focus is always on optimizing the long-term value of each customer relationship - because that's what retention is actually for.
